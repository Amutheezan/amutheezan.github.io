Amutheezan is a 4<sup>th</sup>-year **Ph.D. student** who joined the [Resilient Networks and Systems (RNS) Lab](https://aronlaszka.com/) in Fall 2019.
His research interests lie in the fields of Artificial Intelligence. He holds a **Master's degree in Computer Science** from the University of Houston, which he earned in Summer 2022 alongside his Ph.D. He received his **Bachelor's degree in Computer Science and Engineering** from the University of Moratuwa, Sri Lanka, in 2017. Before starting his Ph.D., he gained work experience as a Software Engineering Intern at WSO<sub>2</sub> Lanka (PVT) Ltd, where he was part of the Data-Analytics Team from July 2016 to December 2016. He then worked as a Software Engineer at MillenniumIT Software (PVT) Ltd, where he was part of the Post-Trade Team from January 2018 to July 2019.

### Interests
* Combinatorial Optimization
* Deep Reinforcement Learning
