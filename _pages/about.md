Amutheezan is a Ph.D. student who joined the [Applied Artificial Intelligence Lab](https://aronlaszka.com/) in Fall 2019. His research focuses Application of Artifical Intelligence to Solve Combinatorial Optimization Problems related to Transportation Domains. He earned a Master's degree in Computer Science from the University of Houston in Summer 2022 while pursuing his Ph.D. He completed his Bachelor's degree in Computer Science and Engineering at the University of Moratuwa, Sri Lanka, in 2017.

Before starting his Ph.D., he gained industry experience as a Software Engineering Intern at WSO<sub>2</sub> Lanka (PVT) Ltd, working with the Data-Analytics Team from July to December 2016. He later worked as a Software Engineer at MillenniumIT Software (PVT) Ltd, contributing to the Post-Trade Team from January 2018 to July 2019.

### Research Interests
* Artificial Intelligence
* Optimization
* Deep Reinforcement Learning
