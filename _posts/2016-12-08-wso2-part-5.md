### **Problem Encountered & Things I learnt**

#### **Arbitrary Attribute**

We can't directly use the arbitrary attribute as it is commonly available as an arbitrary data map. The way to get
specific arbitrary attribute is,

```cast(map:get(ArbitraryMap,'relatedfield/key'), 'typeOfKey')``` as ```fieldName```, here ```typeOfKey``` can be
string,long,int,double. ```fieldName``` is used for ease of access.

```cast(map:get(ArbitraryMap,'relatedfield/key'), 'typeOfKey')``` can be used to filter the input streams based on our
requirement.

##### **Dealing with jaggery controller with CApp**

We can use jaggery controller at dashboard rather than deploying it at ```/portal/controllers/apis```.

##### **Template manager**

Template manager is used for complex event processing related concepts mainly, it is best practice for making the
custom ```publisher```, ```executionplans```, ```eventstore```, ```eventstream```.
