### Contributions

* Introduced mathematical formulation to optimize the energy consumption of public transit agencies that operates mixed fleets
of Electric (EV) and Internal Combustion Engine (ICEVs) Vehicles.
* Transformed the mathematical formulation to solve the problem using integer programming to obtain optimal solution.
* Introduced solution approach comprised of heuristics and meta-heuristics to solve larger problem instances in polynomial time.

### Publications

The work is published in Proceedings of the AAAI Conference on Artificial Intelligence [[AAAI-21]](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/17752).
