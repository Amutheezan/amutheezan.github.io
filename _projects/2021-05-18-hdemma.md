### Contributions

* Formulated the mathematical model to optimize the energy consumption of public transit agencies that operates mixed fleets
of Electric (EV) and Internal Combustion Engine (ICEVs) Vehicles.
* Solution approach comprised of heuristics and meta-heuristics to solve larger problem instances in polynomial time.
