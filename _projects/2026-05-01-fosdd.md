### 📌 Key Contributions

* Developed a retrieval-augmented, multi-agent framework to detect deception in text containing figurative language (sarcasm, irony, metaphor)
* Designed a two-stage architecture combining online inference with an offline knowledge construction pipeline
* Built large-scale annotation pipelines for deception and figure-of-speech labels, producing 1,500+ labeled samples across multiple definition combinations
* Performed evaluation and robustness analysis to identify failure modes across figurative language types
