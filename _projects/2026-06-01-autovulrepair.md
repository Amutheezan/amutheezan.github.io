### 📌 Key Contributions

* Built a multi-agent Repairer-Verifier pipeline to automatically detect and repair vulnerabilities in LLM-generated code
* Assigned specialized model roles (Repairer and Verifier) and integrated retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) to improve repair quality
* Achieved an 88.4% verifier-accepted repair rate, with results validated through manual review and statistical testing (McNemar's exact test)
