### 📌 Key Contributions

* Investigated the effectiveness of leading label-error detection methods (CINCER, AUM, SimiFeat, CleanLab, DynaCor) under real-world noise conditions
* Designed controlled experiments introducing duplicate/near-duplicate samples and label alterations across four real-world datasets spanning vulnerability detection, sarcasm, and deception domains
* Proposed ensemble-agreement-based correctness proxies, validated against human-annotated ground truth
